Jaeyeul Kim

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at DGIST, South Korea, conducting research on autonomous driving under the supervision of Prof. Sunghoon Im at the DGIST Computer Vision Lab.

Specifically, I have worked on LiDAR-based segmentation, detection, tracking, and forecasting. My current research interest is in autonomous vehicle planning. From April to October 2024 I was a visiting researcher at the Robotics Institute, Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, US.
